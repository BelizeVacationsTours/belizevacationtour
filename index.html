Eco-Friendly Tours

Rooted in Hopkins Village

Jaguar Preserve Tours is a locally owned and operated family business based in Hopkins Village. We grew up exploring these trails, rivers, and Maya heritage sites, and we love sharing the stories, traditions, and hidden gems that make Belize unforgettable.

Every itinerary is crafted with the owner's hands-on experience, sustainability in mind, and a genuine commitment to the communities and protected areas we visit. When you travel with us, you support Belizean families and help preserve the habitats we proudly call home.
